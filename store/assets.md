# pShare listing assets

Supply genuine screenshots from the tested release, with personal information removed. Do not use mockups as evidence of unimplemented behavior.

- Store icon: 512 by 512 pixels, 32-bit PNG, at most 1024 KB. An adaptive launcher XML is not a store PNG.
- Feature graphic: 1024 by 500 pixels, JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha.
- At least two screenshots, JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha; dimensions 320 to 3840 pixels and largest side no more than twice the smallest. Check form-factor-specific requirements.

Existing graphics remain at their original project paths; see the assets list in docs/PUBLISHING.md (relative to the project/module root). Missing assets must be captured or designed; none are fabricated here.

Official specifications: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151
